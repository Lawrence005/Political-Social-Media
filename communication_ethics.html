Hi! I’m Joyce, a BSND student who is learning more about nutrition, health, and the importance of communicating information responsibly. As a student and social media user, I’m also learning to look beyond what I see online and think critically about the messages being shared.

For this blog, we will be exploring communication ethics and strategies in political social media through a post involving VP Sara Duterte. Through this activity, I hope to better understand how communication can influence people’s thoughts, emotions, and opinions.

Account: Inday Manila Bulletin

HER RESPONSE

SHE QUESTIONED THE EVIDENCE BEHIND THE IMPEACHMENT EFFORT AND DESCRIBED THE IMPEACHMENT EFFORT AS POLITICAL.

NOTE:
AN ALLEGATION OR COMPLAINT DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY MEAN THAT SOMEONE IS GUILTY.

WHAT DOES THE MESSAGE DO?

THE MESSAGE

IT PRESENTS SARA DUTERTE'S SIDE AND CHALLENGES THE BASIS OF THE IMPEACHMENT COMPLAINT.

THE EMOTION

THE STATEMENT MAY MAKE SUPPORTERS FEEL SYMPATHY OR FRUSTRATION, WHILE CRITICS MAY FEEL DOUBTFUL OR UNCONVINCED.

THE MISSING SIDE

THE POST MAINLY COMMUNICATES HER PERSPECTIVE, SO VIEWERS NEED TO LOOK AT THE COMPLAINANTS’ STATEMENTS AND SUPPORT DOCUMENTS AS WELL.

THE IMPORTANT QUESTION

ARE WE ACCEPTING THE MESSAGE BECAUSE OF THE EVIDENCE—OR BECAUSE OF HOW EFFECTIVELY IT IS COMMUNICATED?

STOP. CHECK. THINK. VERIFY.

CRITICAL THINKING MEANS LOOKING BEYOND ONE POST BEFORE ACCEPTING ITS CLAIMS AS TRUE.

COMMUNICATION
ETHICS
TRUTHFULNESS

HER CLAIMS ABOUT THE COMPLAINT CAN BE CHECKED AGAINST THE ORIGINAL DOCUMENTS AND CREDIBLE REPORTS.

FAIRNESS

THE POST MAINLY PRESENTS HER SIDE, SO THE OPPOSING SIDE AND ADDITIONAL CONTEXT SHOULD ALSO BE CONSIDERED.

RESPECT & DECENCY

CRITICISM SHOULD FOCUS ON THE ISSUE RATHER THAN UNNECESSARILY ATTACKING THE PEOPLE INVOLVED.

ACCOUNTABILITY

SHE DIRECTLY RESPONDED TO THE IMPEACHMENT COMPLAINT AND EXPLAINED HER POSITION.

RESPONSIBILITY

POLITICAL STATEMENTS CAN INFLUENCE PUBLIC OPINION, SO BOTH THE COMMUNICATOR AND THE AUDIENCE HAVE A RESPONSIBILITY TO AVOID SPREADING MISLEADING INFORMATION.

COMMUNICATION
STRATEGIES
DENIAL

HER CLAIMS ABOUT THE COMPLAINT CAN BE CHECKED AGAINST THE ORIGINAL DOCUMENTS AND CREDIBLE REPORTS.

COUNTERATTACK

HER RESPONSE ALSO CRITICIZES THE PEOPLE OR GROUP BEHIND THE IMPEACHMENT EFFORT.

FRAMING

THE MOST PROMINENT STRATEGY IS FRAMING BECAUSE THE ISSUE IS PRESENTED AS A POLITICALLY MOTIVATED EFFORT RATHER THAN SIMPLY AN IMPEACHMENT COMPLAINT.

EMOTIONAL APPEAL

THE STATEMENT CAN ENCOURAGE FEELINGS OF SYMPATHY, FRUSTRATION, OR ANGER.

SELECTIVE PRESENTATION

THE MESSAGE EMPHASIZES INFORMATION SUPPORTING HER POSITION, WHICH MAKES IT IMPORTANT TO EXAMINE OTHER AVAILABLE INFORMATION.

LOOKING BEYOND THE POLITICS

THE WAY PEOPLE INTERPRET POLITICAL MESSAGES CAN BE INFLUENCED BY THEIR EXISTING BELIEFS AND LOYALTY.


A SUPPORTER OF SARA DUTERTE MIGHT VIEW THE STATEMENT AS A STRONG DEFENSE AGAINST POLITICAL ATTACKS. AN OPPONENT MIGHT INTERPRET THE SAME MESSAGE AS AN ATTEMPT TO SHIFT ATTENTION AWAY FROM THE ACCUSATIONS.

CHECK THESE:
  • THE ORIGINAL IMPEACHMENT COMPLAINT
  • OFFICIAL GOVERNMENT DOCUMENTS
  • STATEMENTS FROM BOTH SIDES
  • EVIDENCE SUPPORTING THE CLAIMS
  • CREDIBLE NEWS REPORTS
WHAT I LEARNED

THIS ACTIVITY TAUGHT ME THAT POLITICAL COMMUNICATION IS NOT ONLY ABOUT WHAT A PERSON SAYS, BUT ALSO HOW THE MESSAGE IS PRESENTED. STRATEGIES SUCH AS FRAMING, DENIAL, COUNTERATTACK, AND EMOTIONAL APPEAL CAN INFLUENCE HOW PEOPLE UNDERSTAND AN ISSUE.


I LEARNED THAT I SHOULD NOT IMMEDIATELY BELIEVE A POLITICAL POST JUST BECAUSE IT SOUNDS CONVINCING OR BECAUSE I SUPPORT THE PERSON WHO POSTED IT. I SHOULD CHECK RELIABLE SOURCES, CONSIDER DIFFERENT PERSPECTIVES, AND SEPARATE FACTS FROM OPINIONS.


I ALSO LEARNED THAT ETHICAL COMMUNICATION REQUIRES TRUTHFULNESS, FAIRNESS, RESPECT, AND RESPONSIBILITY. AS A BSND STUDENT, THIS IS ALSO IMPORTANT BECAUSE COMMUNICATING INFORMATION RESPONSIBLY IS PART OF BEING A RESPONSIBLE COMMUNICATOR.
